## Sampling methodology (golden_set.csv)

The taxonomy was derived from **exploratory clustering on a 500-thread development sample** 
before the golden set was assembled. The taxonomy was frozen before any golden-set labelling began.

**Sampling distribution (200 examples)**:
- 50% stratified across the 10 intents (≥10 per intent for common intents)
- 20% from rare intents: `content_availability`, `account_security`, `service_outage`
- 15% ambiguous or multi-intent messages (secondary_intent is populated)
- 10% safety-sensitive / escalation-heavy cases (PII, threats, security)
- 5% random untouched sample (g046–g050, g096–g100 range)

**Labelling protocol**:
- Primary annotator labelled all 200 using the frozen `configs/intent_taxonomy.yaml`
- 50 examples (g001–g050) were re-labelled independently 2 weeks later to assess self-consistency
- Cohen's kappa reported in `artifacts/metrics.json` under `annotation_agreement`
- Disagreements were resolved to the primary label, but original secondary labels preserved

**Leakage prevention**:
- Split is at the **conversation level** (conversation_id), not tweet level
- All 200 examples are in the `test` split — training uses pseudo-labelled threads
- Golden examples are **excluded from the retrieval index** at inference time
- No threshold tuning was performed on the golden test set
